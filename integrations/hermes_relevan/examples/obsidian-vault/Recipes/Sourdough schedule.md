---
title: Sourdough schedule
tags: [recipes, baking]
---
Feed the starter at 08:00 with 1:5:5. Mix at 12:00: 500 g flour, 350 g water, 100 g starter, 10 g salt.
Four sets of stretch and folds every 30 minutes, bulk until 75 percent rise, shape, then cold retard
overnight. Bake at 250 C in the Dutch oven, 20 minutes lid on and 25 minutes lid off.
